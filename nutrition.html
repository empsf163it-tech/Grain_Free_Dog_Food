Transition Guide

7 Days to a happier, healthier gut.

Switching dog foods too quickly can cause temporary digestive upset. Follow our 7-day gradual transition plan for smooth adaptation.

Days 1 - 2

25% WildBowl

Mix 25% WildBowl kibble with 75% of your dog's current food.

Days 3 - 4

50% WildBowl

Increase to a 50/50 balanced blend of old and new food.

Days 5 - 6

75% WildBowl

Feed 75% WildBowl with only 25% of their old formula remaining.

Day 7+

100% WildBowl

Your dog is now fully transitioned to pure, grain-free nutrition!
