About WildBowl

Premium pet food without the mystery.

Founded by veterinary nutritionists and passionate dog owners, WildBowl was built to challenge commercial pet food norms through uncompromising ingredient clarity and protein-first formulations.

Our Founding Story

Born from curiosity and care for canine health.

In 2021, our founders set out to answer a simple question: "Why is it so hard to understand what's actually inside a bag of dog food?" Most commercial brands hide behind vague terms like 'animal derivatives' or bulk up recipes with low-cost corn and wheat fillers.

We created WildBowl to be different. We formulate every single batch with deboned real meats as the primary ingredient, complex grain-free carbohydrates, and 100% transparent labelling—so you know exactly what fuels your dog's vitality.

Quality Assurance

Our 5-Step Farm-to-Bowl Quality Process.

How we guarantee fresh, nutrient-dense kibble in every bag.

Step 1

Ethical Sourcing

100% cage-free poultry, grass-fed lamb, and wild Pacific salmon sourced directly from regional farms.

Step 2

Flash Freezing

Fresh proteins are flash-frozen upon harvest to seal in essential amino acids and natural aroma.

Step 3

Gentle Steam Cooking

Slowly cooked at optimal temperatures to kill pathogens while preserving heat-sensitive vitamins.

Step 4 & 5

Probiotics & Lab Test

Coated with live probiotics post-cook and triple-tested by independent labs before shipment.

Safety & Certifications

ISO 22000 & AAFCO Certified Standards.

Every WildBowl recipe is formulated alongside board-certified veterinary nutritionists to meet AAFCO Dog Food Nutrient Profiles for All Life Stages.

Each production run undergoes rigorous 3-stage safety testing for purity, pathogen control, and guaranteed nutrient stability before leaving our kitchen.
